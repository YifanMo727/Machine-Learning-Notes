1曲线的斜率随位置变化 · Slope Changes Along a Curve ⭐

上一讲的直线处处斜率相同;本讲重点:曲线在不同点的斜率不同。以 f(a) = a² 为例:

同一条曲线,a=2 处斜率是 4,a=5 处斜率是 10 —— 斜率本身是 a 的函数
d/da (a²) = 2a
  • a = 2:f = 4 → 轻推到 2.001 → f ≈ 4.004(变化 ≈ 4 倍)✓
  • a = 5:f = 25 → 轻推到 5.001 → f ≈ 25.010(变化 ≈ 10 倍)✓
For f(a) = a², nudging a at 2 moves f about 4× as much, but at 5 it moves f about 10× as much. The derivative 2a is itself a function of a.

Each formula can be verified by nudging a by 0.001 and watching how much f moves — e.g., a³ at a=2 goes from 8 to ≈8.012, i.e. 12× the nudge.

3两个要点 · Two Takeaways

  • ① 导数就是斜率(高/宽),曲线上不同点的斜率可以不同
  • ② 导数公式不用自己推——查微积分教材/公式表即可(2a、3a²、1/a…)
Takeaway 1: the derivative is just the slope, which can differ at different points. Takeaway 2: you can simply look up derivative formulas in a calculus textbook.
