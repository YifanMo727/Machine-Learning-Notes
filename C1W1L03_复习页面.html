核心知识点 Key Points

1经济价值几乎都来自监督学习 · Value from Supervised Learning

目前神经网络创造的经济价值,几乎全部来自监督学习 (supervised learning):学习一个从输入 x 到输出 y 的映射。

Almost all the economic value created by neural networks so far has been through supervised learning: learning a mapping from input x to output y.

Online advertising is one of the most lucrative applications of neural networks today: predicting whether a user will click on an ad.

3三种主要网络结构 · Types of Neural Networks

  • Standard NN 标准神经网络:一般的结构化数据(房价、广告)
  • CNN 卷积神经网络:通常用于图像数据 (image data)
  • RNN 循环神经网络:用于序列数据 (sequence data)——音频是一维时间序列,语言中单词按顺序出现
  • Hybrid 定制/混合结构:复杂应用(如自动驾驶)会组合多种网络
Standard NNs for structured data; CNNs typically for images; RNNs for sequence data like audio and language; complex applications use custom hybrid architectures.

4结构化 vs 非结构化数据 · Structured vs Unstructured Data

📊 结构化数据 Structured

  • 数据库型数据 (databases of data)
  • 每个特征含义明确:面积、卧室数、用户年龄、广告 ID…
  • 短期内创造经济价值最多(广告、推荐系统)

🎨 非结构化数据 Unstructured

  • 音频、图像、文本 (audio, images, text)
  • 特征是像素值、单个词,无表格含义
  • 深度学习让计算机在这类数据上突飞猛进
Historically it was much harder for computers to make sense of unstructured data. Thanks to deep learning, computers are now much better at speech recognition and image recognition — but much of the short-term economic value still comes from structured data.
